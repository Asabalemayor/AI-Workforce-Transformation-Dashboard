# 🌍 AI Workforce Transformation Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **Onyx Data Challenge** to analyze the impact of Artificial Intelligence on the global workforce between **2021 and 2024**.

Using **Python** for data preparation and **Power BI** for visualization, the dashboard explores AI adoption trends, workforce displacement, job creation, reskilling investment, industry readiness, and country-level AI maturity.

The goal is to provide business leaders and policymakers with actionable insights into how AI is reshaping the labor market and where strategic workforce investments are most needed.

---

## 🎯 Challenge Objectives

This dashboard answers the following business questions:

- How has AI adoption changed before and after the Generative AI era (Q4 2022)?
- Is job displacement accelerating or stabilizing over time?
- Which industries are underprepared for AI disruption?
- Which skill categories have the largest reskilling gaps?
- How do countries compare in AI adoption and workforce readiness?
- Is reskilling investment keeping pace with workforce displacement?

## 📈 Key Insights

- AI adoption accelerated significantly after the emergence of Generative AI (Q4 2022).
- Overall job displacement exceeded job creation throughout the study period.
- Manufacturing and Public Health showed high automation susceptibility but relatively low AI investment.
- Manual Skilled Trades exhibited the largest reskilling gap.
- Countries with stronger digital infrastructure did not always experience lower workforce displacement.
- Reskilling investment was uneven across industries and countries, highlighting preparedness gaps.
